## 6. Imagery direction

**Shoot / select for:** real training intensity; Greystanes/local pitch truth; ages 5–18 without implying everyone is SPL; coach presence when consented; parent-readable moments as well as action.

**Avoid:** stock trophy lens-flare; product flat-lays of boots/balls/apparel for sale (**VOIDED**); esports overlays; unconsented identifiable minors in paid ads (process UNKNOWN — escalate).
